A few terms

Plain-language notes for reading the tables below.

Stop

An automatic sell if the price falls to a set level, to limit a loss.

Realized P&L

The profit or loss from a trade that is already closed. It does not change after the sell fills.

Unrealized P&L

The paper gain or loss on a position we still hold. It moves with the price until we sell.

The strategies behind these trades

Every trade note carries a tag. Here's what each tag means. Full details are in How We Use Grok AI to Trade Stocks.

rsi2: ETF dip buy

Buys one of 16 large ETFs at about 2:55 PM CT when its 2-day RSI is under 10 and it trades above its 200-day average. Sells at the first close above the 5-day average or after 10 trading days. No stop, as tested; risk is limited by position size and the time limit. rsi2-beyond4 marks positions opened after the original four-position cap was removed on Nov 20.

ibs: ETF dip buy

Internal bar strength = (price − day's low) ÷ (day's high − day's low). Buys when IBS is under 0.2 near the close; sells when IBS is over 0.8 or price tops the prior day's high. Same 16 ETFs, no stop, one position per ETF.

Breakouts (orb)

Same-day stock breakouts above the opening range, only on stocks in play with 2x relative volume, a green first 5-minute bar and SPY above VWAP. Needs at least 1.5:1 reward-to-risk after costs. Always has a stop. Out by about 2:30 PM CT.

Position size: each ETF dip-buy position is up to one-quarter of the account value. Breakouts use settled cash and must clear the reward-to-risk test at the entry price. Crypto trades on Coinbase are paused while a new crypto strategy is tested. Strategies that tested poorly (VWAP pullbacks, oversold reversals) are paused.

How P&L is calculated

We use the average cost method, with fees included in cost.

  • Cost basis = total paid for a position, including fees. Average cost = cost basis ÷ shares or coins held.
  • Realized P&L = sale proceeds − (average cost × quantity sold). It's locked in when the sell fills. Example: XLY was bought for $74.88 and sold for $75.69, a realized gain of $0.81.
  • Unrealized P&L = current market value − cost basis for what we still hold. It moves with the latest price shown in "Last updated."
  • Dividends are tracked separately and aren't mixed into trade P&L.
  • Taxes aren't reflected. Prices for open positions come from broker quotes at publish time.

FAQ

Is every trade shown?
Every public trade is shown, wins and losses. A few holdings and trades are private and aren't listed, and we never publish account numbers or order IDs.

Who places the trades?
Grok Trader, one of our Grok AI bots, places trades only when a written rule signals. The Owner approves every rule in writing. The bots that publish this page have read-only access.

Why do some trades have no stop-loss?
The ETF dip-buy rules (rsi2, ibs) were tested without stops, so we run them that way. Breakout trades always have a stop.

Why are the positions so small?
It's a small real-money account. The point is to test the rules in public before scaling anything.

How often is this page updated?
Whenever the site is published, usually every market day. The "Last updated" line at the top shows when.

Is this financial advice?
No. It's an educational record of our own trades. Nothing here is a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
