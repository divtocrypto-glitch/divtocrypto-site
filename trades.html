A few terms

Plain-language notes for reading the tables below.

Stop

An automatic sell if the price falls to a set level, to limit a loss.

Realized P&L

The profit or loss from a trade that is already closed. It does not change after the sell fills.

Unrealized P&L

The paper gain or loss on a position we still hold. It moves with the price until we sell.

Open positions

What we still hold. Unrealized P&L is the paper gain or loss until a position is sold.

Full trade history

All public trades, newest first. Some holdings and trades stay private and are not listed here.

Closed trades

Finished positions only, with realized P&L — the profit or loss after the sell filled.
