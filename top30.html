How the score works (0–100)

  • Yield (up to 30): scales up to 9%. Above 12% we subtract 3 points per extra percent, and above 15% another 3 per percent (6 total), because very high yields usually signal a cut ahead.
  • Track record (up to 25): years of monthly payments, capped at 25 years.
  • Stability (up to 30): starts at 30, minus 12 for each cut or suspension in the last 10 years.
  • Size (up to 15): market cap / fund size on a log scale ($0.1B = 0, $50B+ = 15).

Yield = latest monthly payout × 12 ÷ price. Prices and payouts are from market data on 2026-11-04; track-record and cut counts are approximate from company and fund histories. ETF and royalty-trust payouts vary month to month. Only names currently paying monthly are included.
