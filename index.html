How it works

Four steps. The Owner approves anything that goes public.

  1. 1

    Dividend stocks pay us monthly

    We hold stocks that send a cash dividend every month. That income is the starting point.

  2. 2

    We reinvest into crypto

    Those payouts are put into crypto we follow, instead of being taken as cash.

  3. 3

    AI bots find and trade setups with stops

    A small bot team researches ideas and takes small positions.

    A stop is an automatic sell if the price falls to a set level, to limit a loss.
  4. 4

    Every result is logged here

    Wins, losses, skipped trades, and the public portfolio are posted so you can follow along.
