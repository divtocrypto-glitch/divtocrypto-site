XCN Weekly Roundup (Nov 16–22, 2026): Four Flat Days, Then a Break Below $0.003

By DivToCrypto · Nov 22, 2026 · XCN Weekly Roundups · 5 min read

This roundup covers XCN (Onyxcoin) from Monday, November 16 through our last snapshot on Saturday, November 21, 2026. It replaces the six short daily notes we published this week. It also looks back across all three weeks since we began tracking on November 5, because the bigger picture is clearer than any single day.

Starting with this post, we've retired the daily XCN updates. Each week now gets one roundup like this. The live price is always on the XCN dashboard.

Educational, not financial advice. This is a factual log of a token we hold. It is not a recommendation to buy, sell or stake XCN, and it makes no price predictions.

The week in one paragraph

After the previous week's spike and reversal, XCN went almost completely still. For four mornings in a row, from Monday to Thursday, it moved less than 1.6% a day and stayed between $0.003087 and $0.003114. Then on Friday, November 20, it fell 4.08% to $0.002987, our first snapshot below $0.003. It slipped another 1.67% on Saturday to $0.002937. Across the week, that's a drop of about 4.9%. (Update: Sunday morning's snapshot, added after this post went up, ticked back to $0.002982, up 1.52% from Saturday but still under $0.003.)

Price action: compression, then a slip

Monday through Thursday was the calmest stretch we've recorded. The four snapshots sat inside a range of less than 1%. Wednesday's move was +0.03%, practically zero. Volume was light too, at $2.7M to $3.9M on three of those days.

Quiet periods like this are often described as compression: buyers and sellers in balance, waiting for a reason to move. When the move came, it was down. Friday's -4.08% happened on low volume ($2.3M), which suggests there wasn't much demand underneath. Saturday's volume rose to $8.0M as the price slipped further. A down move on rising volume is worth noting, though one day of data doesn't make a trend.

Market cap followed price, from about $122M on Monday to $116M on Saturday.

Our position

We made no XCN trades this week. The position stayed at 89,688 XCN with a cost basis of $324.91, an average cost of about $0.003623 per XCN. Our trading cash this week went into the separate ETF dip-buy book instead. Those trades are on the Trades page and explained in How We Use Grok AI to Trade Stocks.

At Saturday's snapshot, the XCN position was worth $263.39, an unrealized loss of $61.52, or about -18.9%. Getting back to break-even would take a rise of about 23.3% from $0.002937. That gap has widened each week: about 17.8% at the end of week one (on the smaller, pre-dip-buy position) and 19.2% at the end of week two.

From our first snapshot on November 5 ($0.003430) to Saturday ($0.002937), XCN is down about 14.4%. Three things stand out:

  1. Every week closed lower than it opened. No single day did the damage. It was a slow grind with one failed rally in the middle.
  2. Volatility came in bursts. Two of the three weeks averaged under 2% a day. The middle week, with the 11.9% spike, averaged almost 5%.
  3. Volume only spiked once. Most days traded 2% to 6% of market cap. The Nov 11 spike, at 15%, is the outlier that didn't stick.

What outside commentators said

The YouTube channel C-Zar Gets Crypto posted an XCN video on November 18 (watch). These are his opinions, summarized in our words:

  • He saw a possible double bottom forming with XCN near $0.00315, and said a large further drop in Bitcoin was his main worry.
  • He considered the pattern intact above roughly $0.0028, with $0.0027 as a last line before it looked weaker.
  • He expected a slow, U-shaped recovery with consolidation first, and thought a stronger move might start in early 2027 rather than this year.

For the record: Saturday's snapshot of $0.002937 was below the $0.00315 he quoted but still above the $0.0028 level he named. We report that as a chart fact, not an endorsement. Our longer summary of his November 6 call is still up: C-Zar's November 6 XCN Call, Summarized.

News and staking

We found no major official Onyx or XCN news this week. Onyx's published liquid staking rate was still listed at about 30% APR, variable and set by governance (sources: onyx.org, Onyx docs). As we showed in the first roundup, a year at 30% APR is fully offset by a price drop of about 23%. XCN fell about 14.4% in just over two weeks of tracking, which is why we never treat the staking rate as a cushion.
