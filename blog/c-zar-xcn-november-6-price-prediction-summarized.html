C-Zar's November 6 XCN Call: More Room to the Downside, Summarized

By DivToCrypto · Nov 7, 2026 · 4 min read

On November 6, 2026, C-Zar Gets Crypto (speaker goes by Cesar) published XCN OnyxCoin Price Prediction: 6th Of November. This post summarizes the chart case and price opinions he laid out in that video, in our own words, from the video's auto-generated English captions (ASR). It is his analysis, not ours. No new XCN video from Crypto Future appeared in the channel RSS since our last XCN post.

His technical case

In the video, XCN's spot price was about $0.0034, with roughly nine and a half hours left in the November 6 daily candle.

Cesar says that for a while his expectation was that XCN would probably not go below certain recent lows — and that over the past week, those lows were broken. He has shifted that view, and he preemptively warns that it is possible price goes below the current low again, and maybe even back toward prior bear-market low areas. He frames that as common in an accumulation range when a market is turning from lower-lows / lower-highs: a double bottom, a slightly higher low, or a slightly lower low.

He is not saying he fully believes a deeper flush is guaranteed. He wants viewers prepared. In his view, if Bitcoin drops about 2% to 6%, Onyxcoin could drop about 4% to 12% (or more). From the ~$0.0034 area, he sketches that a 4% move would stay above one watched low, while a 12% move would sit just below that low and just above another. If Bitcoin goes lower than he expects, he notes XCN is not far from that next shelf.

If price finds support in that lower zone, he describes a W-looking (double-bottom style) formation as a more constructive structure for accumulation — even if the path there is not a continuous rally.

Other chart points he raised:

  • About six weeks of weekly red so far; he expects the current week to close red and thinks the next week may also be red, with a chance the down-move runs seven to nine weeks before it ends.
  • He does not expect XCN to "drop through the floor"; he still calls this an accumulation area.
  • Next support he watches: about $0.0032, or a Fib zone around $0.00329 to $0.003. If $0.003 does not hold, he looks for a double bottom.
  • 1-hour: RSI and stochastics look more bearish than bullish; MACD inconsistent in chop.
  • 4-hour: RSI dwelling below 50 and getting oversold; stochastics oversold without getting overbought — he calls that a very bearish environment; MACD again inconsistent in sideways action.
  • Daily: oscillators getting oversold — he reads that as a signal that the lower-low / lower-high downtrend in place since about September 27 may be approaching its end on a time basis, while still allowing another ~5–10% of price downside.
  • Target area he keeps circling: just below $0.0033, hopefully just above $0.003. A confirmed double bottom, in his words, would be a powerful reversal signal if it happens.

C-Zar's price opinions

The numbers below are Cesar's opinions from the November 6 video, not DivToCrypto forecasts. Watch the source: XCN OnyxCoin Price Prediction: 6th Of November on C-Zar Gets Crypto.

  • Still believes that by end of November, XCN could see prices as high as $0.0086 (he says larger moves in shorter time have happened before).
  • If not by end of November, he expects that area by end of 2026.
  • Near term: more turbulence is possible; he closes with "buy the dip / hold through scary times" as his framing, not ours.

His November 5 video was more confident that a spinning-top / doji marked the low near ~$0.00356. This November 6 update is more cautious on the path: broken lows, room for another flush, then the same upside opinion later. These calls change from day to day; treat each video as a snapshot, not a fixed plan.
