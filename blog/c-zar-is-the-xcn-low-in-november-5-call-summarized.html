C-Zar: Is the XCN Low In? His November 5 Call, Summarized

By DivToCrypto · Nov 5, 2026 · 2 min read

On November 5, 2026, C-Zar Gets Crypto (about 51,500 subscribers; speaker goes by Cesar) published XCN OnyxCoin Spinning Top Bottom Signal: THE LOW IS IN / Price Prediction: 5th of November. This post summarizes the chart case and price opinions he laid out in that video, in our own words, from the video's captions. It is his analysis, not ours.

His technical case

In the video, XCN's spot price was about $0.00356. Cesar argues that yesterday's spinning-top / doji daily close is a bullish reversal candle, and that the low may already be in or very near.

He leaves room for one more dip: a move toward about $0.00329, or slightly below, is still possible. He does not expect much lower than that if Bitcoin holds around $78k and consolidates.

Other chart points he raised:

  • Volume drying up on the way down, which he reads as constructive.
  • The 1-hour chart showing an unusual inverse head-and-shoulders.
  • The 4-hour still looking more bearish, but breaking its downtrend.
  • The daily oversold, with a possible MACD bullish divergence.

On the bigger picture, he wants a higher high against the long string of lower highs since January 2025. He thinks the first half of a move toward prior highs is possible, with the bigger flourish coming late 2027 into 2028, above prior-cycle highs.

C-Zar's price opinions

The numbers below are Cesar's opinions from the November 5 video, not DivToCrypto forecasts. Watch the source: XCN OnyxCoin Spinning Top Bottom Signal (Nov 5) on C-Zar Gets Crypto.

  • Upside as early as next week or this month.
  • By end of 2026: $0.0086 or higher (he also said it could come faster, even in about two weeks).

Cesar has been posting near-daily XCN analysis. His November 4 video was more cautious: he was not yet convinced the low was in and expected a low within 7–10 days. These calls change from day to day; treat each video as a snapshot, not a fixed plan.

Our take

These are one trader's chart readings, not guarantees. XCN is down and volatile. DivToCrypto's own position is 56,517 XCN held long term with staking. We hold for Onyx's published ~30% variable staking APR, not because of short-term candle calls. Details are on the XCN page.

Risks

XCN is a small-cap crypto token. Price can move sharply in either direction. Technical patterns fail. Influencer price targets are opinions and are often wrong. Staking APR is variable and can be cut. Smart-contract, liquidity, and market risks apply.
