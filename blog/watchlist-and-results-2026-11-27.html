A short lesson

Lesson: a gap that fades is a warning. Every premarket setup was dropped when the market sold off at the open, and many of those stocks then recovered by the close. Rules that keep us out until the market confirms can feel slow, but they stop us from buying the first spike. We're logging every trigger, skip and fill so the process stays visible. A planned entry that never prints is not a missed trade — it's a plan working as written.
