C-Zar's November 8 XCN Video: A Year of Support, Summarized

By DivToCrypto · Nov 9, 2026 · 2 min read

On November 8, 2026, C-Zar Gets Crypto (the host goes by Caesar) published XCN OnyxCoin Price Prediction: 8th of November. This post summarizes his analysis in our own words, based on the video's auto-generated English captions. It is his opinion, not ours. Crypto Future posted no XCN-specific video since our last XCN post.

His chart case

At the time of recording XCN was at about $0.0338 by his reading, with roughly seven hours left in the daily candle. He zoomed out to the weekly chart and argued that XCN is at a pivotal point and a major buying opportunity compared with anything since January 2025.

  • A price area that acted as resistance from December 2023 to January 2025, about two years, has been acting as support since roughly November 2025, so about one year.
  • He reads the past year as an accumulation phase with a rounding bottom, even though lower lows and lower highs have still occurred inside it.
  • He argues that big downtrends usually do not reverse overnight: they tend to go through a boring accumulation period first.
  • He expects XCN to break out of this range sometime within the next year, and says he would not be surprised to see higher highs before the end of 2026.
  • He treats a move above a specific recent high as the signal that the trend of lower highs is over.

His price opinions

These are Caesar's opinions from the November 8 video, not DivToCrypto forecasts.

  • He thinks XCN can still go lower. Using Fibonacci levels from the cycle low to the recent macro high, he marks the 0.786 level near $0.0033 and the 0.886 level near $0.00308, and expects support somewhere in that range.
  • He thinks a rally into the end of 2026 is possible after support holds.
  • He does not expect XCN above the 38.2% retracement zone in early 2027, and says it is possible by the end of 2027. He tells viewers to get comfortable with XCN staying under one cent for a while longer.

Our take

This is one trader's reading of the chart. His calls have shifted from video to video, and his November 6 video was more focused on a possible further flush. We track XCN's live price and Onyx's published liquid staking rate on the XCN dashboard. We are not making a price prediction.

Risks

XCN is a small-cap token and can move sharply either way. Chart patterns often fail, influencer targets are frequently wrong, staking rates are variable, and smart-contract and liquidity risks apply.
